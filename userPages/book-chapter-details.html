Synopsis

Artificial Intelligence and IoT for Intelligent Prediction and Decision Support in Real-World Application is an edited volume that presents the latest developments in Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) for intelligent prediction and decision support. The book explores how data-driven technologies enable accurate forecasting, automation, and real-time decision-making across diverse real-world domains. It discusses the integration of AI with cloud computing, edge computing, Big Data Analytics, Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI), Digital Twins, and IoT-enabled smart systems to develop scalable and efficient predictive solutions.

The chapters combine theoretical concepts with practical applications, demonstrating AI-driven predictive models in healthcare, transportation, electric vehicles, environmental monitoring, smart cities, agriculture, finance, industrial automation, energy management, and higher education. The book serves as a comprehensive reference for researchers, academicians, students, engineers, and industry professionals interested in intelligent predictive analytics and next-generation decision support systems.
