Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for this internship program?

B.Tech, B.Sc and BCA students from any year can apply. It is especially useful if you want to strengthen your technical skills and build a project portfolio.

How do I choose which domain to specialise in?

Choose based on your career interests, academic background or future goals. An orientation session gives an overview of every domain, and mentors can help you decide.

What is the duration and schedule of the internship?

The internship has 30 hours of training spread over 4–6 weeks. Sessions run on weekends or evenings so they fit around college classes. The exact schedule is shared when you enroll.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes. Once you complete the internship and submit your capstone project, you receive an internship certificate from ResNova Academic Press that you can add to your resume and LinkedIn profile.

What kind of capstone project will I work on?

A practical, real-world application in your chosen domain. For example, an object detection system for Computer Vision or a sentiment analysis tool for NLP. You get guidance at every stage.

Do I need prior programming experience?

Basic programming knowledge is recommended but not mandatory. We start with fundamentals. If you are new to programming, try basic Python before the program begins.

Is the internship conducted online or offline?

Online. All sessions are live and interactive with hands-on labs. Recordings and learning materials are available for revision during and after the program.

What support do I get for my resume and LinkedIn profile?

We guide you on presenting your internship and project: writing strong project descriptions, highlighting technical skills and showing your work professionally to recruiters.

Can I interact with mentors and ask questions?

Yes. You can ask questions during live sessions, in dedicated doubt-clearing sessions and on our communication platform.

What payment options are available?

The fee is ₹5,000 for the complete program. You can pay by bank transfer, UPI or online payment gateway. Details are shared during enrollment.
