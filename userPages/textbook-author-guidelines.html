Author guidelines for textbook preparation

ResNova Academic Press invites authors to contribute high-quality textbooks that combine academic rigour with practical insight. These guidelines keep manuscripts uniform, clear and ready for production. Each textbook follows the sequence below.

Preliminary pages

  • Title pageBook title, author(s), affiliation, contact details
  • Preface
  • Acknowledgements
  • Table of contents
  • List of figures and tables
  • List of abbreviations (if applicable)

Main content

Each chapter must include:

  1. Chapter title and number
  2. Learning objectives / outcomes
  3. Introduction
  4. Detailed content with appropriate headings and subheadings
  5. Illustrations, diagrams and case studies
  6. Summary or key takeaways
  7. Review questions, exercises and problems
  8. References (APA 7th or IEEE style)

End matter

  • Glossary of key terms
  • Bibliography
  • Index
  • Appendices (if any)

Citation and referencing

Choose one of the following styles based on your subject discipline.

APA, 7th edition

Kumar, A., & Reddy, S. (2023). Data Analytics Using Python. ResNova Academic Press.

IEEE format

[1] A. Kumar and S. Reddy, Data Analytics Using Python, ResNova Academic Press, 2023.

Requirements and process

What we expect from your manuscript, and what happens after you submit it.

Authorship and biography

  • List all contributors who made significant academic or technical contributions.
  • Include short biographies (50–100 words) with recent photographs.
  • Mention affiliations, email IDs and ORCID IDs (if available).

Originality and plagiarism

  • Manuscripts must be original and unpublished.
  • Similarity index should be below 15% (excluding references).
  • Authors are responsible for obtaining permissions for all third-party materials.

Pedagogical elements

To enhance teaching and learning outcomes, authors are encouraged to include:

  • Learning objectives at the beginning of each chapter.
  • Key terms highlighted in bold.
  • Case studies and real-world examples.
  • Review questions, MCQs and assignments for self-assessment.
  • Illustrations, charts, flow diagrams and concept summaries.

Submission requirements

  • File format: MS Word (.docx)
  • Figures: .jpg / .png / .tif (300 DPI)
  • Equations: MS Equation Editor / MathType
  • Submit a single, well-organised file with properly named figures and tables.

Review and proofing process

  • Each submission undergoes technical review, editorial review and plagiarism screening.
  • Authors receive proofs before final publication.
  • Only minor corrections are accepted at the proof stage.

Copyright and permissions

  • Authors sign a Copyright Transfer Agreement (CTA) upon acceptance.
  • Permissions must be obtained for any reused material, including text, tables or figures.

Optional sections

Authors may include:

  • Instructor resources: slides, question banks, lab manuals.
  • Student resources: datasets, code files, assignments.
  • Online supplementary materials.
