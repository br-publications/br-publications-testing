Our development process

A proven step-by-step approach that helps deliver your project on time, within budget, and beyond expectations.

  1. 1

    Discovery & requirements

    3–5 days

    We start with in-depth consultation to understand your business goals, target audience, and functional requirements. We document all features, create user personas, and define project scope clearly.

  2. 2

    Planning & architecture

    5–7 days

    Our team creates detailed technical specifications, database schemas, API documentation, and system architecture. We select the optimal technology stack and create a development roadmap with milestones.

  3. 3

    UI/UX design

    1–2 weeks

    Designers create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes focusing on user experience. We present multiple design concepts and refine based on your feedback before final approval.

  4. 4

    Development

    4–12 weeks, depending on complexity

    Our developers build your application using best coding practices, working in sprints with regular demos. Both frontend and backend are developed simultaneously with continuous integration.

  5. 5

    Quality assurance & testing

    1–2 weeks

    Comprehensive testing including functionality, performance, security, and compatibility across devices and browsers. We fix all bugs and ensure everything works flawlessly.

  6. 6

    Deployment & launch

    2–3 days

    We handle server setup, domain configuration, SSL certificates, and deploy your application to production. Final checks are performed before the official launch.

  7. 7

    Training & handover

    3–5 days

    We provide comprehensive documentation, admin panel training, and knowledge transfer sessions. You'll receive all source code, credentials, and ongoing support information.

What we need from you

Eight things that keep the project moving

To ensure smooth project execution, we need the following information and materials from your end. If something is not ready yet, such as a domain or third-party accounts, we can help you set it up.

Business requirements document

What
A detailed description of your business goals, target audience, key features you want, and any specific functionalities.
Format
Document, presentation, or detailed email explaining your vision and requirements.

Design preferences & branding

What
Your company logo, brand colors, fonts, style guidelines, and reference websites you like.
Format
Logo files (PNG, SVG), brand guidelines PDF, links to inspirational websites.

Content & media

What
Text content for pages, images, videos, product descriptions, or any multimedia you want to include.
Format
Text documents, high-resolution images (JPG, PNG), videos (MP4, MOV).

User roles & permissions

What
Define different user types (admin, customer, manager etc.) and what each role can do.
Format
List or spreadsheet outlining roles and their specific permissions.

Third-party integrations

What
Any external services to integrate (payment gateways, email services, analytics, APIs).
Format
List of services with API credentials if available, or we can help you set them up.

Domain & hosting details

What
Your domain name (if purchased) and hosting preferences. We can guide you if not yet arranged.
Format
Domain registrar access or we can handle purchase and setup.

Budget & timeline expectations

What
Your budget range and desired launch date or deadline.
Format
Clear communication during initial consultation.

Point of contact

What
Designated person from your team for approvals, feedback, and communication.
Format
Name, email, phone number, and preferred communication method.
